## Full description

Broadway Property Management helps landlords, property managers, and platform operators in Rwanda run portfolios from one place.

**Features**

- Property and unit registry with UPI fields  
- Tenant profiles, leases, and document storage  
- Rent invoices in Rwandan Francs (RWF)  
- Manual MTN MoMo / bank transfer proof upload and review  
- Tax obligation tracking with RRA-oriented disclaimers  
- Multi-role workspaces: owner, accountant, lawyer, tenant, platform operator  
- Real-time dashboard updates  

**Who it is for**

- Landlords and property management firms in Kigali and across Rwanda  
- Accountants and lawyers collaborating on a portfolio  
- Tenants viewing invoices and submitting payment proof
